What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where attackers impersonate trusted entities to steal sensitive information like passwords, credit card numbers, or login credentials.

Types of Phishing Attacks

📧 Email Phishing
Mass emails impersonating banks, services, or companies to steal credentials.
🎯 Spear Phishing
Targeted attack on a specific person using personal information to appear legitimate.
🐋 Whaling
Targets high-level executives like CEOs or CFOs for maximum financial damage.
📱 Smishing
Phishing via SMS text messages with malicious links or fake alerts.
📞 Vishing
Voice calls impersonating banks, IT departments, or government agencies.
🌐 Clone Phishing
Duplicates a real email but replaces links or attachments with malicious ones.

How to Recognize Phishing

🔴
Urgent or threatening language — "Act NOW or your account will be closed!"
🔴
Suspicious sender email — support@paypa1.com instead of paypal.com
🔴
Generic greetings like "Dear Customer" instead of your real name
🔴
Links that look real but redirect to fake sites — always hover before clicking
🔴
Unexpected attachments — especially .exe, .zip, .docm file types
🔴
Requests for passwords, OTPs, or credit card info via email or SMS
🔴
Poor grammar, spelling mistakes, or mismatched logos and branding

Social Engineering Tactics

Phishing works because it exploits human psychology, not just technology.

⏰ Urgency
Creates panic so you act without thinking. "Your account will be deleted in 24 hours!" — designed to bypass rational thought.
👔 Authority
Pretends to be your boss, IT department, bank, or government agency to make you comply without questioning.
🎁 Scarcity
"You've won a prize! Claim it before midnight." Creates fear of missing out (FOMO) to rush your decision-making.
🤝 Familiarity
Uses your name, employer, colleagues, or recent activity to appear trustworthy and bypass suspicion.

Best Practices to Stay Safe

✅
Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on all accounts — stops 99% of credential attacks
✅
Verify sender email addresses carefully — look for subtle misspellings in the domain
✅
Hover over links to preview the actual URL before clicking anything
✅
Never share passwords, OTPs, or PINs via email, call, or text — ever
✅
Report suspicious emails to your IT/security team immediately — don't just delete
✅
Keep your browser, OS, and antivirus software up to date at all times
✅
When in doubt — delete it. No legitimate company ever asks for passwords via email.

Key Takeaways

🎯   Phishing is the #1 cyberattack method — anyone can be a target, not just companies
🔍   Always verify the sender, inspect links, and question urgency before acting
🛡️   Enable MFA — it alone blocks 99% of credential-based phishing attacks
🚨   Report suspicious messages to your security team — don't just delete and ignore
🧠   Security is a mindset, not just a tool — stay alert, stay skeptical, stay safe
